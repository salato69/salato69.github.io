Cuty Planner

Commitments, tabung and daily spending in one place. Private to your Google account and synced across your phone and computer.

  • Plan your incomeAllocate your salary to rent, loans and bills each month.
  • Grow your tabungSave for emergencies, Hari Raya, a wedding or a trip.
  • Stay within your daily limitLog what you spend and see right away if you went over.

Your data is stored privately in your own account. Nobody else, including the app's owner, can see it in the app.
